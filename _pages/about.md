I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow in the [Gaynor Lab](https://gaynorlab.weebly.com/) at the University of British Columbia, supported by the [AI for Biodiversity Change Global Center](https://www.abcresearchcenter.org/). I am interested in projects bridging the fields of wildlife ecology and computer vision. I recently completed my PhD in Wildlife Science at Oregon State University, where I worked on <a href="/projects/bioacoustics">bioacoustics</a> for monitoring northern spotted owls and computer vision methods with camera traps for monitoring mammal communities in <a href="/projects/oregon_critters">Oregon</a> and <a href="/projects/nkhotakota_camera_traps">Malawi</a>. Previously I studied habitat ecology and distribution of North American <a href="/projects/porcupines">porcupines</a> in the Pacific Northwest.
